What would you write down if your city was on fire? 🔥

On 2 September 1666, a maid woke Samuel Pepys at three in the morning to say a fire had started in Pudding Lane. He glanced out of the window, decided it was far away... and went back to bed.

A few hours later, it wasn't far away at all.

So he climbed the Tower of London, took a boat along the river, told the King the only way to stop it was to pull houses down, and buried his wine and his Parmesan cheese in a pit. Through all of it, he kept writing.

Over 13,000 houses and 87 churches were lost. But his diary survived, and because he wrote it all down, we can stand in that room today.
